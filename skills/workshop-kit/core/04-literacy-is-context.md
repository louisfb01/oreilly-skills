## AI literacy is the skill of managing context
- The model does not get smarter during your chat. What changes is what is on the desk.
- Good results come from putting the right things in and taking the wrong things out.
- Every habit in the rest of today is a way to manage context.
> visual: a clean desk with three labelled sheets (task, source, format) versus a cluttered one
> notes: This is the central idea of the workshop. Come back to it in every exercise.

## What goes in, what goes out
- In: the task, the source files, the format you want, one good example.
- Out: old tasks, off-topic turns, outdated versions of the same file.
> visual: two columns, "In" and "Out", as cards
